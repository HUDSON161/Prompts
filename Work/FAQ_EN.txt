> Question 6:
How does ::: Auto Lot ::: work when trading multiple pairs simultaneously?

> Response Essence (Main Idea):
 ::: Auto Lot ::: automatically distributes the total portfolio risk among all active pairs and timeframes. It accounts for the total number of instruments and divides the lot so that the total load on the deposit doesn't exceed the specified parameters. For correct operation on live accounts, it's recommended to always keep this setting enabled.

> Question 7:

> Question 14:
Why do you place such emphasis on diversification across many pairs?

> Response Essence (Main Idea):
Diversification is the only reliable way to smooth the equity curve and reduce drawdowns without losing potential profit. By trading dozens of pairs simultaneously, we eliminate situations where an error on a single instrument critically affects the entire deposit. A portfolio approach makes trading more stable over long distances.

> Question 18:

> Response Essence (Main Idea):

Instead of trying to limit the loss on one trade, we strive for the superiority of the system's mathematical expectation over distance.
